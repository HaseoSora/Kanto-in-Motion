# Kanto in Motion v1.4.0 — Gen 1 HD

v1.4.0 is a major Gen 1 presentation update for **Pokémon Red / Blue / Yellow** on Gen1Recomp.

## Highlights

- **HD animated Pokémon #001–151** with front/back, normal/shiny, and available male/female variants.
- **1920×950 HD battle backgrounds** with location-aware sunrise/day/sunset/night routing and static cave/interior handling.
- Integrated **move animations for all 165 Gen 1 moves**, including fullscreen BG/FG effects and move SFX.
- **Modern Battle UI** with configurable move layout, move info, battle text size, panel size/opacity, and HUD controls.
- Configurable **shiny odds**, retained shiny identity after capture, and a one-shot sparkle/audio cue.
- Configurable **Pokémon shadows** with quality and opacity controls.
- Updated title-screen presentation with **TITLE PKMN SIZE** for the HD Pokémon artwork.
- Updated desktop/mobile compatibility for **Battle Art 1.11.x** and **PotatoVoxel**, including HD Pokémon, KIM move animations, trainer handoff, 3D sizing, HUD/QOL alignment, party Poké Balls, and Modern lower battle UI.
- Corrected **EXP/already-caught overlay alignment** across the current KIM, Battle Art, and PotatoVoxel paths.

## Compatibility and scope

- Target: **Gen1Recomp Gen 1** (`>=0.2.24 <0.3.0`).
- Games: **Pokémon Red / Blue / Yellow**.
- **Gen 2 support is not included in v1.4.0.**
- Battle Art and PotatoVoxel are optional external compatibility mods and are not bundled.
- The internal mod ID remains `animated_menu_pokemon`, allowing compatible Kanto in Motion settings to carry forward.
