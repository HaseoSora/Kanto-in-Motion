# Kanto in Motion v1.4.1 package

This is the **public Gen 1 HD release** of Kanto in Motion v1.4.1, promoted from the confirmed-good Gen1 HD v39 baseline.

The package includes the HD Pokémon #001–151 battle pipeline, 1920×950 time-of-day battle backgrounds, integrated Gen 1 move animations/SFX, Modern UI/HUD presentation, shiny effects, and the confirmed Battle Art/PotatoVoxel desktop/mobile compatibility paths.

Gen 2 support is intentionally not included in this release.
