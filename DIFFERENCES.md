# v1.4.0 scope differences

Compared with the older Kanto in Motion v1.3.7 release line, v1.4.0 moves the Gen 1 battle presentation to the new HD pipeline.

- Gen 1 is the active game target for this release; Gen 2 support is not included.
- HD animated Pokémon #001–151 replace the older generation-selection battle-art pipeline.
- 1920×950 location/time-aware HD battle backgrounds replace the older legacy Gen 6/Battle Art arena selection.
- The integrated 165-move Gen 1 Kanto Rework / Pokémon Essentials-style animation system is restored.
- Battle Art 1.11.x and PotatoVoxel use dedicated KIM compatibility paths on desktop and mobile without modifying either external mod.
- Modern battle UI, KIM HUD controls, shiny effects, battle trainer selection, title presentation, Poké Ball fixes, and animated Trainer Card badges remain integrated.
- Custom Trainer Card player/Gym Leader portraits are not included; animated badges remain.
